# 🍓 Prisha’s Daily

A blush-pink planner for your top three, your little routines, and the days you want to remember.

**🌷 [Website address](https://srivastavaprisha29-ops.github.io/Prisha-s-Daily-/)** · GitHub Pages publishing is pending.

## 🎀 What’s inside

- **Today’s top three.** Pick three priorities and tick them off as you go.
- **Habit Trail.** Add your own habits to a winding board. Ivory tiles turn pink with a checkmark when you finish them.
- **Activity garden.** Tasks and habits light up a 13-week green grid. Select a date to revisit its checkmarks.
- **Planner extras.** A monthly schedule, notes, goals, checklists, and a player for your own audio files.

## 🍒 Try it locally

Download the repository and serve it with `python3 -m http.server 8000`,  open `http://localhost:8000`.

Built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Your entries are saved in the browser you use.
